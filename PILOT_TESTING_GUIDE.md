# VertiGoes pilot testing guide

## Before each tester starts

- Use the production PWA at https://app.vertigoes.co/.
- Explain that this is a pilot, not a medical device or emergency service.
- Ask testers to use fictional or low-sensitivity test information during the first round.
- Do not place a real emergency call. Opening and closing the phone dialer is sufficient.
- Provide one monitored contact channel in addition to **Menu → Other → Report a bug**.

## Suggested first pilot

Start with 5–10 invited adults using a mix of Android Chrome and iPhone Safari. Ask each
tester to complete the checklist below, then collect feedback before expanding the group.

## Tester checklist

1. Open the app and add it to the home screen.
2. Switch between English and Hebrew and check layout direction and truncation.
3. Create an account, confirm the email, sign out, and sign in again.
4. Complete all onboarding pages, including the six consent choices and safety questions.
5. Close and reopen the installed PWA and confirm onboarding does not restart.
6. Save a daily mood check-in with text; optionally test a short voice note.
7. Save sleep or another available daily record, reload, and confirm it remains visible.
8. Open the Emergency area, verify its explanation, and close it without making a call.
9. Submit a test report from **Menu → Other → Report a bug**.
10. Use a disposable account to test **Menu → Danger zone → Delete account**.

## Feedback questions

1. What device, operating system, and browser did you use?
2. Could you install and reopen the app from your home screen?
3. Where did you hesitate or become confused?
4. Did any saved answer disappear or show an error?
5. Was any text clipped, too small, or incorrectly aligned?
6. Did the safety and medical limitations feel clear?
7. What is the single most important improvement before a larger pilot?

For every defect, record the time, screen, action, expected result, actual result, device,
browser, language, and whether retrying fixed it. Do not include passwords, access tokens,
or real health details in screenshots or reports.
