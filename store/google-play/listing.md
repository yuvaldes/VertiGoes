## Full description

VertiGoes is a private companion for adults living with dizziness, vertigo and balance symptoms.

Use VertiGoes to:

- record daily dizziness-related events;
- track sleep and mood over time;
- keep optional health, medication and safety information with your profile;
- prepare clearer notes for conversations with qualified healthcare professionals;
- access safety guidance and your chosen emergency contact when you need it.

Your account data is protected by account-level access controls. VertiGoes does not sell your information or use advertising identifiers. You can delete your account and associated health data from inside the app.

Some features are still being developed and are clearly marked as coming soon.

Important medical disclaimer: VertiGoes is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any medical condition. It is not a substitute for examination, diagnosis, treatment or a rehabilitation plan from a licensed clinician. It does not monitor your condition or summon emergency help. In an emergency, contact local emergency services immediately.
