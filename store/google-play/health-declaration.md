# Google Play Health apps declaration draft

Declare that the app provides health features. Based on the currently enabled release, select:

- Diseases and Conditions Management
- Sleep Management
- Stress Management, Relaxation, Mental Acuity

Do not select Medical Device Apps. VertiGoes does not claim to diagnose, treat, cure or prevent a condition and the store description contains the required non-medical-device disclaimer.

If exercise/rehabilitation content is enabled in a later release, update the declaration to add Physical Therapy and Rehabilitation before publishing that update.

The privacy policy is available both publicly and inside the app:

https://app.vertigoes.co/privacy.html
