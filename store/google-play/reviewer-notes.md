# Google Play reviewer notes

VertiGoes is an adult health-record companion and is not a medical device. It does not diagnose, provide medical treatment, monitor emergencies or contact emergency services.

## Access

The Home screen and legal documents can be reviewed without an account. Account-backed health records require authentication. Provide a dedicated reviewer email/password in Play Console's App access section; do not place credentials in this repository.

## Permissions

Microphone permission is optional and requested only after a signed-in user taps **Add voice note** in the daily mood form. Denying permission does not block the rest of the app. Audio remains on the user's device and is not uploaded by the current implementation.

## Account deletion

Signed-in path: **Menu → Danger zone → Delete account**.

External instructions: https://app.vertigoes.co/account-deletion.html

## Emergency feature

The Emergency navigation action offers safety guidance and a user-provided contact shortcut. It is not an emergency monitoring or dispatch service. The app directs users to local emergency services for emergencies.
