Delete your VertiGoes account

VertiGoes lets you delete your account and associated data at any time.

Delete inside the app

  1. Sign in to VertiGoes.
  2. Open Menu.
  3. Under Danger zone, select Delete account and confirm.

Request deletion without the app

Email us from the address registered to your VertiGoes account. Use the subject “Delete my VertiGoes account.” We may ask you to verify ownership before processing the request.

Request account deletion

What is deleted

Your account, profile, onboarding and health answers, calendar history, mood records, and other account-linked health data are deleted. Voice-note audio stored locally remains on the device until the app or its storage is removed.

What may be retained

An anonymized consent or security record may be retained where required to demonstrate compliance or prevent abuse. It is not retained as an active account and does not include your symptom history. Backup copies may remain briefly until normal rotation completes.
