Privacy Policy

VertiGoes · Last updated: 27 September 2026 · Version 1.0

VertiGoes stores personal details and health information to provide the app. We do not sell information, trade it, or provide it to advertisers. You may request deletion of your information at any time.

Data controller and contact

Maya Caspy, founder of VertiGoes, is the owner of the app and database and the data controller. For privacy questions, requests, or complaints, email vertigoesmaya@gmail.com.

Information we collect

  • Account details: email address and password. Passwords are handled by Supabase Authentication as one-way hashes.
  • Optional profile details: name or nickname, gender, and preferred interface language.
  • Optional sensitive health information: diagnoses, medications, safety answers, sleep, mood scores and tags, text notes, episode diary entries, symptoms, severity, and exercises or manoeuvres logged.
  • Optional voice notes: if you choose to record a mood note, the app asks for microphone access. The audio remains in the app's storage on your device; a local file reference may be included in your synced daily record, but the audio file is not uploaded to VertiGoes.
  • Optional emergency contact: a name and telephone number shown for you to dial from your device. We do not contact that person.
  • Consent record: the accepted consent version and timestamp.
  • On-device storage: profile, consent record, and sign-in token, so the app can work offline and retain your session.

VertiGoes does not use advertising or advertising identifiers, request location, contacts, photos, or camera access, or track you across other apps or websites. Microphone access is requested only when you choose to create an optional voice note.

Health data and legal basis

Health data is sensitive data. Before health data is stored, VertiGoes asks for separate, explicit consent to store and process it for the purpose of running the app. The consent is not preselected and is recorded with a version and timestamp. We rely on your explicit consent for this processing, including under Article 9(2)(a) of the GDPR. Other account information is processed to provide the service you request.

You can withdraw consent by emailing us. Because the app relies on this information, withdrawal is treated as a request to delete your account and information. Withdrawal does not affect processing that was lawful before it was withdrawn.

Storage, processors, and transfers

Your information is stored with Supabase, acting as our data processor, on servers in the European Union. Row Level Security is used so database rows are accessible only to the account that created them. Supabase is bound by a Data Processing Agreement and may not use your information for its own purposes.

We do not currently provide personal or health data to other providers. We may disclose information if required by law or court order. Supabase is a US company and support staff may access data outside the EU for support subject to its data-processing terms and Standard Contractual Clauses. VertiGoes operates from Israel, which the EU recognizes as providing an adequate level of protection.

Retention and deletion

Account information is kept while your account exists. On a deletion request, personal and health information is removed from the database within 30 days. Consent records may be retained longer as evidence of consent; they do not contain symptom or health details. Backup copies may retain information briefly until rotated.

Your rights

Depending on applicable law, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction or objection to processing, portability, and withdrawal of consent. You can edit profile details in the app. For deletion or any other request, email us from your registered email address; we aim to respond within 30 days at no charge. You may also complain to the Israeli Privacy Protection Authority or, in the EU, your national supervisory authority.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest, passwords are stored as hashes, and administrative keys are not included in the app. No system is completely secure. If an incident is likely to affect your rights, we will notify you and relevant authorities promptly. Protect your device, since someone with access to an unlocked device may see information in the app.

Children

VertiGoes is not intended for anyone under 18. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

We may update this policy as the app develops. The current version and date will appear on this page. If we expand the health data we collect or change its purpose, we will request fresh explicit consent before the change takes effect.
